Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Best One Golf ("App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Songpol Rungsawang, handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

The App allows you to enter game data such as player names, team names, golf scores, and game settings. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We do not collect personal information directly. However, the following third-party services integrated into the App may collect certain data:

  • Advertising Services: The App may display ads through third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob). These services may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.
  • App Store Analytics: Apple App Store and Google Play Store may collect usage and crash data as part of their standard analytics. This is governed by their respective privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

All game data (player names, scores, settings, and settlement calculations) is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. We do not have access to this data. The data is used solely to provide the App's core functionality: tracking golf scores and calculating settlements.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All user-created data is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate servers that store your personal or game data.
  • If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted.
  • We recommend backing up your device regularly to prevent data loss.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob - for displaying advertisements
  • Apple App Store - for app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Google Play Store - for app distribution and in-app purchases

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases (e.g., ad removal). These transactions are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

7. Your Choices

  • Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy > Advertising; Android: Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all App data by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through your device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
